I made a mod that puts a tablet with your Twitch chat into the driver's cab of
RUNNING TRAIN.

[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/xdghcnt/RUNNING-TRAIN-twitch-tablet-mod/main/docs/screenshot-day.jpg[/img]

It is an object in the cab, not an overlay: it rides with the train and sits
where you put it, like a phone clipped to the desk, so you can glance at chat
without leaving the cab view. Names come in their Twitch colours and emotes as
pictures, your channel's own ones included. Chat is read anonymously - no
login, no token, you only type your channel name into the config.

Positions for hr1500, hr1100, kr5000 and DC8500 are included. In any other
train the tablet appears right in front of you; move it into place with the
numpad and save, once per train. Font size, screen brightness and all the
keys can be changed, so it also works without a numpad.

[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/xdghcnt/RUNNING-TRAIN-twitch-tablet-mod/main/docs/screenshot-night.jpg[/img]

It works alongside my [url=https://steamcommunity.com/app/4630570/discussions/0/583931831085175535/]head tracking mod[/url] - install both in any order.

[b]Download and full instructions:[/b] https://github.com/xdghcnt/RUNNING-TRAIN-twitch-tablet-mod

Install is a single drag-and-drop into the game folder. No installer, no extra
runtime. Everything - setup, key bindings, config, troubleshooting - is in the
readme at the link.

The game is in Early Access, so a patch may eventually break this. It is free
and open source (MIT), and feedback is very welcome, especially if you
calibrate the tablet in a train I haven't - send me your numbers and I'll add
them as a preset.
